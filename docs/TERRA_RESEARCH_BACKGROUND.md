# Terra Research Background

The platform organizes five research directions without claiming novelty from their mere inclusion:

1. Run/liquidation mechanisms: asset-price breaks interact with redemption constraints and liquidity.
2. Wallet withdrawal hazard: time-to-exit can depend on depeg, concentration, flows, backing, and cohort features.
3. Network contagion: rolling dependence across UST, LUNA, BTC, ETH, and DeFi assets is a secondary P2 analysis.
4. Terra/LUNA simulation: protocol mint/burn, market execution, liquidity depletion, and confidence form a feedback system.
5. ABM stress testing: heterogeneous delays, thresholds, capital, and information response can generate nonlinear aggregate outcomes.

The defensible contribution is one reproducible cutoff-safe workflow that compares simple and complex models against isolated ground truth, then tests mechanisms and counterfactuals.
