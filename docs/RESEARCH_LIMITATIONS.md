# Research Limitations

- Historical archival endpoints and indexed exports may be incomplete.
- Wallet intent and confidence are latent; on-chain actions reveal behavior only imperfectly.
- CEX order-book and off-chain venue data may be unavailable.
- Terra conversion, aggregate liquidity, execution, reserve action, and settlement risk require simplification.
- Missing intervals and uncertain source alignment may affect reconstructed hourly state.
- Cox PH is not defensible without wallet event durations; the current fallback is labelled accordingly.
- Fixture results validate software behavior, not the historical event.
- Out-of-sample historical replay is retrospective and is not live prospective prediction.
- Historical Terra validation does not validate Aave liquidation mechanics; the mechanisms are separate.
- Model-implied probabilities are conditional on the model and are not objective real-world probabilities.
