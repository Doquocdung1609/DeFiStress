Historical outcome labels may be reconstructed later under approval, but must not leak into predictor inputs or threshold selection.

Fit transforms/scales/imputers only on train; seal warm-up, missingness, all required features, delay, vintage and common-support masks. No random split. ## Frozen comparison and metrics

Register B0/B1/B2/B3 (and any later methods separately), identical information sets, decision clocks/horizons and independently defined common eligible support. Publish per-model coverage and paired common-mask results. Freeze all configuration/source/label/model/profile hashes and sensitivity variants before case scoring. Any model selection uses train/calibration only; log outcome access and version changes, retain unsuccessful methods and abstentions.

For each horizon, a true advance alert must be issued before the registered onset and within the horizon. Signed lead=`event_onset-issued_at`, including issue/detection delay uncertainty; late alerts have negative lead and cannot predict initial instability. A miss has no lead, remains in the event denominator and is never dropped from average lead. Report first matched valid alert and all predeclared sensitivity endpoints; an always-on remote alarm is not success.

Proposed episode rule: adjacent eligible alert decisions belong to one episode; an eligible NORMAL decision terminates it. ABSTAIN does not terminate an episode or count as all-clear, and exposure gaps/censoring are reported. A gap/maximum-span rule must be sealed if the calendar contains unavailable periods; no hidden reset. Match episodes/events by the frozen horizon/one-to-one matching policy. Count raw alert observations and false-alert episodes separately. Publish eligible non-event exposure, registered exposure, abstentions/censoring, event denominators and each exclusion.
